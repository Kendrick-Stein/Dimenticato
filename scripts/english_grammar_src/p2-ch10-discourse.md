=== t01-cohesion | B2
# 1．衔接与连贯：指代、连接词与信息结构

一篇好文章不只是正确句子的堆砌，句与句之间还要“连得上”（衔接，cohesion），整篇的意思要“说得通”（连贯，coherence）。英语依靠**指代词、连接词、词汇复现**和**已知信息在前、新信息在后**的信息结构把句子串成语篇。中国学生写作常见的问题是：连接词堆砌（Firstly... Secondly... What's more... Besides...），代词指代不清，或每句话都从新话题开始，读起来跳跃。

## 核心规则

### 1．指代（reference）

- 用 **he / she / it / they / this / that / these / such** 回指前文，避免重复。
- **this + 名词** 比单独的 this 更清楚：This **decision** angered many staff.
- 代词应指向最近、最明显的名词；若有两个可能的先行词，宁可重复名词。

### 2．连接词（linking devices）

| 逻辑关系 | 常用连接词 | 语体 |
|---|---|---|
| 递进 | also, moreover, in addition, furthermore | moreover / furthermore 较正式 |
| 转折 | however, nevertheless, on the other hand, yet | yet 可作连词 |
| 因果 | therefore, as a result, consequently, thus | thus 较正式 |
| 举例 | for example, for instance, such as | such as 后接名词 |
| 让步 | although, even though, despite, admittedly | despite 后接名词 / 动名词 |
| 总结 | in short, overall, to sum up | on the whole 偏口语 |

- **however, therefore** 是副词，不能像 and, but 那样连接两个分句；前面要用句号或分号：..., **; however,** ...
- 连接词要**少而准**，一段中两三个就够了；逻辑清楚时可以不用。

### 3．信息结构（given-new）

- 句首放**已知信息**，句尾放**新信息**，下一句再以上一句的新信息开头，形成“链式推进”：
  The city built a new **tram line**. **This line** now carries 50,000 **passengers** a day. **Most of these passengers** used to drive.
- 为了把新信息放到句尾，可以灵活使用被动语态、there be 或分裂句。

### 4．词汇衔接

- 使用**同义词、上下义词**回指：the car → the vehicle；apples → the fruit。

## 例句

- **The company cut its prices last year. As a result, sales rose sharply.** 公司去年降价了，结果销量大幅上升。
- **Many people want to live near the coast. However, housing there is expensive.** 许多人想住在海边，然而那里的房价很高。
- **The survey was small; nevertheless, its findings are worth noting.** 这项调查规模不大，不过其结果值得关注。
- **We tested three designs. The second one proved the most durable.** 我们测试了三种设计，第二种被证明最耐用。
- **Researchers studied 200 families. These families were followed for ten years.** 研究人员研究了 200 个家庭，对这些家庭进行了十年的跟踪。
- **The river flooded twice in May. This problem had been predicted by local experts.** 这条河五月发了两次洪水，当地专家早就预测过这个问题。
- **Online courses are flexible; on the other hand, they require strong self-discipline.** 网课灵活；但另一方面，也需要很强的自律能力。
- **Tigers are endangered. The animal has lost most of its habitat.** 老虎濒临灭绝，这种动物已失去大部分栖息地。

## 常见错误

- 误：*The traffic was heavy, however, we arrived on time.*。正：**The traffic was heavy; however, we arrived on time.**。however 是副词，不能用逗号连接两个完整句子。
- 误：*Besides, the rent is high. What's more, the rooms are small. Moreover, it is noisy.*。正：**The rent is high and the rooms are small. Worse still, the street is noisy.**。连接词堆砌显得机械，考官会扣分；逻辑清楚时少用连接词。
- 误：*My brother met my father's friend, and he gave him a book.*。正：**My brother met my father's friend, who gave him a book.**。两个 he 指代不清，改用关系从句或重复名词。
- 误：*Although the plan was good, but nobody supported it.*。正：**Although the plan was good, nobody supported it.**。汉语“虽然……但是”成对出现，英语只用其一。
- 误：*On the contrary, city life has some advantages.*（想表示“另一方面”）。正：**On the other hand, city life has some advantages.**。on the contrary 用于否定前一说法，不等于 on the other hand。

## 学习提示

写完段落后做“主语检查”：把每句话的主语连起来读一遍，看它们是否形成一条清楚的线索。如果每句主语都毫无关系，就说明信息结构断了，需要调整语序或加指代。连接词不是越多越高级，“逻辑自然 + 少量精准的连接词”才是高分作文的特征。

=== t02-nominalization | C1
# 2．名词化与正式语体

名词化（nominalisation）是把动词、形容词所表达的动作或性质转换成名词短语，例如把 The government decided to... 改写为 The government's decision to...。它是学术写作、公文和新闻报道最显著的特征之一，能让句子信息更密集、语气更客观。汉语多用动词，中文思维写出的英文往往“动词串动词”，显得口语化；但过度名词化又会让文章晦涩难懂，因此关键在于把握分寸。

## 核心规则

### 1．常见名词化方式

| 词类 | 原词 | 名词化形式 |
|---|---|---|
| 动词 | analyse / fail / reduce | analysis / failure / reduction |
| 动词 | discover / arrive / refuse | discovery / arrival / refusal |
| 形容词 | important / accurate / available | importance / accuracy / availability |
| 形容词 | difficult / stable / aware | difficulty / stability / awareness |

### 2．名词化带来的句式变化

- 动作的执行者变成**所有格或 of 短语**：The prices rose. → **the rise in prices**
- 副词变成**形容词**：The economy grew rapidly. → **the rapid growth of the economy**
- 从句变成**名词短语**，可以作主语或宾语：Because the funding was cut, ... → **The cut in funding** led to ...
- 因果关系由连词变成**动词**：because → cause, lead to, result in；so → result from, be due to。

### 3．正式语体的其他特征

- 少用第一、二人称，多用 **it is + 形容词 + that**、被动语态。
- 用单个正式动词替代短语动词：find out → **discover**，put off → **postpone**，look into → **investigate**，go up → **increase**。
- 避免缩写（don't → do not）、口语词（a lot of → a great deal of / considerable）和模糊词（thing, stuff）。

### 4．过度名词化的风险

- “名词堆叠”会让句子失去生气：*The implementation of the evaluation of the reduction of costs* 令人费解。
- 原则：**关键概念用名词，动作性强的内容仍用动词**；一个句子里的 of 短语最好不要超过两个。

## 例句

- **The rapid expansion of cities has put pressure on water supplies.** 城市的快速扩张给供水带来了压力。
- **The committee's refusal to comment fuelled speculation.** 委员会拒绝置评，引发了更多猜测。
- **A reduction in working hours may lead to higher productivity.** 缩短工作时间可能带来更高的生产率。
- **The availability of cheap flights has transformed tourism.** 廉价航班的普及改变了旅游业。
- **Failure to comply with these rules will result in a fine.** 不遵守这些规定将被罚款。
- **There has been a marked increase in online shopping.** 网购显著增长。
- **The delay was due to a shortage of qualified staff.** 延误是由于合格员工短缺造成的。
- **Growing awareness of climate change has influenced consumer choices.** 人们对气候变化的认识日益加深，影响了消费选择。

## 常见错误

- 误：*Because people used cars too much, so the air got worse.*。正：**Heavy car use has led to a decline in air quality.**。既犯了 because...so 连用的错误，又过于口语化；名词化后因果关系由 led to 表达。
- 误：*The develop of technology is fast.*。正：**The development of technology is rapid.**。动词不能直接作名词用，要用正确的名词形式。
- 误：*There is a big increase of prices.*。正：**There is a sharp increase in prices.**。increase / rise / fall / reduction 表示“在某方面”的变化时接 in，形容变化幅度用 sharp、significant 等。
- 误：*The making of the decision of the approval of the budget was delayed.*。正：**The decision to approve the budget was delayed.**。过度名词化导致 of 堆叠，应适当还原为不定式或动词。
- 误：*We found out that a lot of stuff was wrong.*（学术写作）。正：**The study revealed several significant errors.**。正式语体避免短语动词、a lot of 和 stuff。

## 学习提示

练习名词化的有效方法是“双向改写”：把一段口语化的句子改成名词化版本，再把一段学术论文里的名词化句子改回动词版本，比较两者在长度、语气和清晰度上的差别。雅思 Task 1 描述图表时，a steady rise in、a sharp drop in、a significant proportion of 等名词化表达几乎是必备的。

=== t03-parallelism | C1
# 3．平行结构与句子平衡

平行结构（parallelism）要求用 and, or, but, both...and, not only...but also, either...or 等连接的成分在**语法形式上保持一致**：名词对名词、不定式对不定式、从句对从句。它不仅是语法规则，也是修辞手段，能让句子节奏清晰、说服力更强。汉语的并列往往不要求词性一致（“学习、工作和身体健康”），因此中国学习者容易写出 *I like reading, to swim and go hiking* 这类不平衡的句子。

## 核心规则

### 1．并列成分形式一致

- 名词 + 名词：She values **honesty, patience and humour**.
- 动名词 + 动名词：He enjoys **cooking, gardening and painting**.
- 不定式 + 不定式（第二个 to 可省略，但全句须统一）：We came **to learn** and (to) **share**.
- 从句 + 从句：I know **what you said** and **why you said it**.

### 2．关联连词的对称

- **both A and B / either A or B / neither A nor B / not only A but also B / whether A or B** 中，A 与 B 结构应相同，关联词应放在被连接成分的正前方。
  误置：She **not only** plays the piano **but also** the violin.
  平衡：She plays **not only** the piano **but also** the violin.

### 3．比较中的平行

- 比较的对象必须对等：**Living in a city** is more expensive than **living in a village**.
- 使用 than, as 时注意前后成分同类：The salary here is higher than **that** in my hometown.

### 4．句子平衡与尾重

- 长而重的成分放在句尾（end weight），必要时用形式主语 it：**It is** unclear **whether the project will continue**.
- 三项并列（rule of three）是演讲和广告中常见的修辞，如 **faster, safer and cheaper**，能制造鲜明的节奏感。

| 不平衡 | 平衡 |
|---|---|
| reading, to write and speaking | reading, writing and speaking |
| quickly, careful and with accuracy | quickly, carefully and accurately |
| She is smart and has kindness. | She is smart and kind. |
| either by bus or we can walk | either by bus or on foot |
| to plan is better than acting | planning is better than acting |

## 例句

- **The course teaches students to think critically, write clearly and speak confidently.** 这门课教学生批判性地思考、清晰地写作、自信地表达。
- **Success depends not only on talent but also on persistence.** 成功不仅取决于天赋，也取决于坚持。
- **She would rather walk to work than wait for the bus.** 她宁愿走路上班，也不愿等公交。
- **The new policy is cheap, simple and effective.** 新政策成本低、简单而有效。
- **Whether you agree or disagree, please explain your reasons.** 无论你赞同还是反对，都请说明理由。
- **Teaching a child to read is harder than teaching a child to swim.** 教孩子读书比教孩子游泳难。
- **We need to reduce waste, reuse materials and recycle whatever we can.** 我们需要减少浪费、重复利用材料，并尽可能回收。
- **It remains unclear who approved the payment and why it was never questioned.** 目前还不清楚是谁批准了这笔付款，以及为什么从未有人质疑。

## 常见错误

- 误：*My hobbies are reading, to travel and I also like cooking.*。正：**My hobbies are reading, travelling and cooking.**。三个并列成分统一为动名词。
- 误：*He is not only a good doctor but also teaches well.*。正：**He is not only a good doctor but also a good teacher.**。not only 与 but also 后应接同类成分。
- 误：*The weather in Harbin is colder than Guangzhou.*。正：**The weather in Harbin is colder than that in Guangzhou.**。比较对象应是“天气”对“天气”，而不是“天气”对“城市”。
- 误：*She either will call you or send an email.*。正：**She will either call you or send an email.**。either 应紧挨着第一个并列成分 call。
- 误：*The job requires patience, being flexible and to have good communication.*。正：**The job requires patience, flexibility and good communication skills.**。三个并列成分统一为名词短语。

## 学习提示

检查平行结构时，把 and / or / but 两边的成分竖着写下来，一眼就能看出形式是否一致。修改时优先选择最简洁的形式，通常是名词或形容词。平行结构还能大大提升作文开头和结尾的气势，例如用三个平行的不定式短语概括全文观点，但要确保内容真正并列，而不是为了凑数。

=== t04-chinglish | B2
# 4．中国学习者常见病句总结

这是全书的“查错清单”。汉语与英语在冠词、名词复数、时态、主谓一致、句子边界和关联词上存在系统性差异，这些差异会反复导致同类错误，也就是“汉语负迁移”。本节把最常见的病句按类型归纳，每类给出判断方法，适合在写作完成后逐条核对，也适合考前集中复习。

## 分类查错清单

| 类别 | 典型病句 | 修正 |
|---|---|---|
| 冠词 | I am student. | I am a student. |
| 名词复数 | many informations | a lot of information |
| 时态 | Yesterday I go to the gym. | Yesterday I went to the gym. |
| 主谓一致 | He don't like it. | He doesn't like it. |
| 句子边界 | I was tired, I went to bed. | I was tired, so I went to bed. |
| 关联词 | Because it rained, so we stayed in. | Because it rained, we stayed in. |
| 垂悬修饰语 | Walking in the park, a dog bit me. | While I was walking in the park, a dog bit me. |
| 词性 | I very like music. | I like music very much. |

### 要点说明

- **冠词**：可数名词单数前必须有 a / an / the / my 等限定词；泛指复数、不可数名词不加 the（Life is short）。
- **不可数名词**：information, advice, equipment, furniture, homework, news, luggage, research 没有复数，不用 a / many。
- **时态**：汉语靠“了、过、昨天”表示时间，英语必须改变动词形式；有明确过去时间用一般过去时，不用现在完成时。
- **主谓一致**：第三人称单数加 -s；everyone, each, the number of 接单数；a number of 接复数。
- **逗号粘连（comma splice）与连写句（run-on sentence）**：两个完整句子不能只靠逗号连接，要用句号、分号或 and / but / so / because 等连词。
- **成对关联词**：although / though 不与 but 连用，because 不与 so 连用。
- **垂悬修饰语**：句首分词短语的逻辑主语必须是主句主语。
- **there be 与 have 混用**：*There have many people* 应为 There are many people。
- **中式搭配**：learn knowledge 应为 acquire / gain knowledge，open the light 应为 turn on the light。

## 例句

- **She is an engineer at a small firm.** 她是一家小公司的工程师。
- **Could you give me some advice about the interview?** 你能给我一些面试方面的建议吗？
- **I visited my grandparents last weekend.** 我上周末去看望了祖父母。
- **Everyone in my class has a laptop.** 我们班每个人都有笔记本电脑。
- **The shop was closed, so we went home.** 商店关门了，所以我们回家了。
- **Although the test was hard, most students passed.** 尽管考试很难，大多数学生还是通过了。
- **Looking out of the window, I saw a rainbow.** 我往窗外看时，看到了一道彩虹。
- **There are a lot of people in the square.** 广场上有很多人。

## 常见错误

- 误：*Although he is rich, but he is not happy.*。正：**Although he is rich, he is not happy.**。汉语“虽然……但是”要成对，英语二者只能选其一。
- 误：*I like this city very much, the people here are friendly.*。正：**I like this city very much because the people here are friendly.**。逗号不能连接两个独立句子，这是最常见的“逗号粘连”。
- 误：*Having finished the homework, the TV was turned on.*。正：**Having finished my homework, I turned on the TV.**。分词短语的逻辑主语应与主句主语一致，电视不会写作业。
- 误：*The number of students are increasing.*。正：**The number of students is increasing.**。the number of 的中心词是 number，谓语用单数。
- 误：*I have seen the film last night.*。正：**I saw the film last night.**。有明确的过去时间状语 last night，不能用现在完成时。
- 误：*We should learn more knowledge.*。正：**We should gain more knowledge.**。英语中 learn 通常接技能或具体内容，knowledge 常与 gain、acquire 搭配。

## 学习提示

建议建立个人“错题本”：每次作文批改后，把错误按上表八类归档，一个月后统计哪一类最多，针对性复习。写作时先完成内容，再按清单做三遍专项检查：第一遍查名词（冠词、单复数），第二遍查动词（时态、主谓一致），第三遍查句子边界和关联词。坚持一段时间，大部分中式错误都会明显减少。

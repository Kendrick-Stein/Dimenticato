=== t01-object-clauses | A2
# 1．宾语从句

宾语从句是在复合句中充当宾语的从句，最常见于 think、know、say、hope、wonder、ask 等动词之后。掌握宾语从句要抓住三件事：**连接词、语序、时态**。中国学习者常在从句里保留疑问语序，或者忽略主句与从句的时态呼应。

## 核心规则

### 连接词

- **that**：引导陈述句，本身无意义，在口语和非正式文体中常省略：I think (that) she is right.
- **if / whether**：引导一般疑问句，意为"是否"：I'm not sure **if** he will come.
- **连接代词** who、whom、whose、what、which 与 **连接副词** when、where、why、how：引导特殊疑问句：Tell me **what** you need.

### 语序

- 宾语从句一律用**陈述语序**：She asked me where **I lived**，不说 where did I live。

### 时态呼应

- 主句为现在时或将来时，从句时态按实际需要选择。
- 主句为**过去时**，从句一般用相应的过去时态（现在 → 过去，将来 → 过去将来，现在完成 → 过去完成）。
- 从句陈述**客观真理、科学事实**时，保持一般现在时：The teacher said that light travels faster than sound.

| 主句 | 原句 | 宾语从句 |
|---|---|---|
| He says | "I am tired." | he is tired |
| He said | "I am tired." | he was tired |
| He said | "I will call." | he would call |
| He said | "I have lost it." | he had lost it |
| He asked | "Do you smoke?" | if I smoked |
| He asked | "Where is it?" | where it was |

### 介词和形容词后的宾语从句

- 介词后可接 wh- 从句或 whether 从句：We argued about **where we should go**.
- sure、afraid、glad、sorry、aware 等形容词后可接 that 从句：I'm afraid **(that) we're sold out**.

## 例句

- **I believe that honesty always pays off.** 我相信诚实终有回报。
- **She told me she had already booked the tickets.** 她告诉我她已经订好了票。
- **Do you know who left this note?** 你知道这张字条是谁留的吗？
- **I'm not sure whether the shop accepts cards.** 我不确定这家店收不收银行卡。
- **The guide explained how the ancient canal was built.** 导游讲解了这条古运河是怎样修建的。
- **We were surprised at what he said.** 我们对他说的话感到吃惊。
- **My grandfather often says that time is money.** 我爷爷常说时间就是金钱。
- **I'm glad you could make it tonight.** 很高兴你今晚能来。

## 常见错误

- 误：*I don't know where does he live.*。正：**I don't know where he lives.**。宾语从句用陈述语序，汉语负迁移常导致保留疑问语序。
- 误：*He said he will come tomorrow.*（作为转述过去的话）。正：**He said he would come tomorrow.**。主句为过去时，从句将来时相应后移。
- 误：*I think he doesn't know me.*。正：**I don't think he knows me.**。think、believe 类动词的否定一般前移到主句。
- 误：*She asked me that whether I was hungry.*。正：**She asked me whether I was hungry.**。连接词只能用一个，that 与 whether 不叠用。

## 学习提示

写宾语从句时可以默念三问："用什么词连？语序正不正？时态要不要变？"口语里 that 几乎总被省略，但在 said、reported 等正式动词后，以及从句较长时，保留 that 能让句子更清楚。

=== t02-subject-clauses | B1
# 2．主语从句

主语从句是在句中充当主语的从句。由于从句作主语往往"头重脚轻"，英语更习惯用 **it 作形式主语**，把真正的主语从句放到句末。中国学习者常省略 that 导致句子无法理解，或者误以为主语从句的谓语要随从句内容变成复数。

## 核心规则

- 引导词：**that、whether、who、what、which、when、where、why、how、whatever、whoever** 等。
- 主语从句在句首时，**that 不能省略**：**That he lied** upset everyone.
- 主语从句在句首时只能用 **whether**，不能用 if：**Whether we win** doesn't matter.
- 主语从句作主语，谓语动词通常用**单数**：What they need **is** more time. 但 what 从句表示复数概念且表语为复数时，也可以用复数：What we saw **were** empty houses.
- 常用 **it 作形式主语**，从句后置：**It is clear that** ... / **It seems that** ... / **It doesn't matter whether** ...

### 常见 it 形式主语句型

| 句型 | 例 |
|---|---|
| It is + 形容词 + that... | It is obvious that she is nervous. |
| It is + 名词 + that... | It is a pity that you missed the show. |
| It is + 过去分词 + that... | It is said that the castle is haunted. |
| It + 不及物动词 + that... | It seems that the plan has failed. |
| It doesn't matter + wh-... | It doesn't matter who pays. |

### what 与 that 的区别

- **what** 在从句中充当成分（主语、宾语或表语），意为"所……的东西"：**What** he said was true.（what 作 said 的宾语）
- **that** 在从句中不充当任何成分，只起连接作用：**That** he said it was surprising.

### 虚拟语气提示

It is important / necessary / essential / vital **that** 后，从句常用 **（should）+ 动词原形**：It is essential that every passenger **(should) wear** a seat belt.

## 例句

- **What you said last night really hurt her.** 你昨晚说的话真的伤了她的心。
- **Whether the event goes ahead depends on the weather.** 活动能否照常举行取决于天气。
- **It is obvious that the team needs a new coach.** 很明显这支球队需要一位新教练。
- **Who will replace the director hasn't been decided yet.** 谁将接替主任还没有定下来。
- **It is reported that the bridge will reopen next month.** 据报道，这座桥下个月将重新开放。
- **How the virus spreads remains unclear.** 这种病毒如何传播目前仍不清楚。
- **Whoever breaks the rules will be fined.** 无论谁违反规定都会被罚款。
- **It is vital that the data be checked before publication.** 数据在发布前必须经过核查。

## 常见错误

- 误：*He passed the exam surprised us.*。正：**That he passed the exam surprised us.** 或 **It surprised us that he passed the exam.**。句首主语从句不能省略 that。
- 误：*If she will come is unknown.*。正：**Whether she will come is unknown.**。if 不能引导句首主语从句。
- 误：*That he needs is a good rest.*。正：**What he needs is a good rest.**。从句缺宾语，需要 what 充当成分。
- 误：*What they told us are lies.*。正：**What they told us is a lie.** 或 **What they told us were lies.**。谓语默认单数，复数须与表语一致且意义明确。

## 学习提示

写作时如果主语从句太长，优先改用 it 形式主语结构，读起来更流畅。判断用 what 还是 that 时，看从句本身是否完整：缺成分用 what，不缺成分用 that。"It is said / believed / reported that..."是学术和新闻写作中的高频句型，值得熟记。

=== t03-predicative-appositive | B2
# 3．表语从句与同位语从句

表语从句跟在系动词之后，说明主语"是什么"；同位语从句跟在某些抽象名词之后，解释这个名词的具体内容。两者都常用 that 引导，形式上与定语从句也很接近，因此是高级阶段的难点。中国学习者最常见的问题是把同位语从句和定语从句混为一谈，或者在 reason、because 的搭配上出错。

## 表语从句

- 结构：**主语 + 系动词（be、seem、look、sound、remain）+ 从句**。
- 引导词：that、whether、what、who、when、where、why、how、as if / as though、because。
- **The reason is that...**：reason 作主语时，表语从句用 that，不用 because。
- **That is why...** 表结果；**That is because...** 表原因，不能颠倒。
- look、seem、sound 后常用 **as if / as though**：It looks as if it's going to snow.

| 句型 | 含义 | 例 |
|---|---|---|
| The problem is that... | 问题在于 | The problem is that we lack staff. |
| The question is whether... | 问题是是否 | The question is whether it works. |
| That is why... | 那就是……的原因（结果） | That is why I left early. |
| That is because... | 那是因为（原因） | That is because I was ill. |
| It seems as if... | 似乎 | It seems as if nobody cares. |

## 同位语从句

- 常见先行名词：**fact、news、idea、belief、hope、thought、possibility、evidence、promise、suggestion、doubt、question、rumour、conclusion**。
- that 在同位语从句中**不作任何成分**，也**不能省略**；从句本身是一个完整句子，解释名词内容。
- 有时从句与名词被谓语隔开：**The news came that the talks had broken down.**
- 表"是否"用 **whether**，不用 if：There is some doubt **whether** the figures are reliable.
- suggestion、order、request 等后的同位语从句常用 **（should）+ 原形**。

### 同位语从句与定语从句的区别

| 比较项 | 同位语从句 | 定语从句 |
|---|---|---|
| 功能 | 解释名词的内容 | 限定、修饰名词 |
| that 的作用 | 只连接，不作成分 | 作主语或宾语 |
| 能否换成 which | 不能 | 通常可以 |
| 例 | the news that she won | the news that she told me |

检验方法：在名词后加 be，若"名词 is 从句内容"成立，一般就是同位语从句：the news is that she won（成立）。

## 例句

- **The truth is that nobody read the instructions.** 事实是没有人看过说明书。
- **The question remains whether the budget will cover the costs.** 问题依然是预算是否够用。
- **That is why online security matters so much.** 这就是网络安全如此重要的原因。
- **It sounds as if the concert has already started.** 听起来音乐会好像已经开始了。
- **The fact that she apologised surprised her colleagues.** 她道歉了，这件事让同事们很意外。
- **There is no evidence that the drug causes memory loss.** 没有证据表明这种药会导致失忆。
- **We welcomed the suggestion that the meeting be moved online.** 我们欢迎把会议改到线上举行的建议。
- **A rumour spread that the factory would close.** 工厂要关闭的传言传开了。

## 常见错误

- 误：*The reason why I was late is because the bus broke down.*。正：**The reason why I was late is that the bus broke down.**。reason 作主语时表语从句用 that，避免语义重复。
- 误：*I heard the news which our team had won.*。正：**I heard the news that our team had won.**。同位语从句只能用 that，which 只能引导定语从句。
- 误：*He made a promise he would never be late again*（书面语）。正：**He made a promise that he would never be late again.**。同位语从句的 that 一般不省略。
- 误：*That is why I was ill.*（想说"那是因为我病了"）。正：**That is because I was ill.**。why 引出结果，because 引出原因。

## 学习提示

记住一对"镜像句"：That is why 后面接结果，That is because 后面接原因。碰到 fact、idea、news 后的 that 从句，先判断从句是否完整：完整就是同位语从句，缺主语或宾语就是定语从句。写议论文时，"The fact that..."和"There is no doubt that..."是增强论证力度的好句型。

=== t04-whether-ever-words | B2
# 4．whether / if 与 whatever / whoever 引导的名词性从句

表示"是否"时，whether 与 if 在很多宾语从句中可以互换，但在其他位置差异明显。而 whatever、whoever、whichever 这类 -ever 词既能引导名词性从句，又能引导让步状语从句，功能交叉，是考试和写作中的高频难点。中国学习者常把 whoever 与 no matter who 混用在不该用的地方，或在介词后误用 if。

## whether 与 if 的分工

- **两者皆可**：及物动词后的宾语从句：I asked **whether / if** she was coming.
- **只能用 whether** 的情况：

| 情况 | 例 |
|---|---|
| 主语从句（句首） | Whether he agrees is another matter. |
| 表语从句 | The issue is whether it is legal. |
| 同位语从句 | the question whether we should go |
| 介词之后 | It depends on whether they pay. |
| 直接接 or not | I don't know whether or not he's in. |
| 接不定式 | She can't decide whether to accept. |

- if 在句中可能产生歧义：**Let me know if you can come.** 既可理解为"告诉我你能不能来"，也可理解为"如果你能来就告诉我"。要表达"是否"并避免歧义时用 whether。
- 正式文体偏好 whether，口语中 if 更常见。

## -ever 词引导的名词性从句

- **whatever** = anything that，**whoever** = anyone who，**whichever** = any one that：这些词在从句中充当成分，整个从句作主语、宾语或表语。
- 作名词性从句时**不能用 no matter + wh- 替换**；no matter who / what 只能引导让步状语从句。
- 注意格：**whoever** 作从句主语，**whomever** 作从句宾语（正式），口语常一律用 whoever。判断依据是它在**从句中**的成分，不是在主句中的位置：Give the prize to **whoever** wins.（whoever 是 wins 的主语）

| 用法 | 名词性从句 | 让步状语从句 |
|---|---|---|
| 含义 | 任何……的人 / 东西 | 无论……都…… |
| 可否换 no matter | 不可以 | 可以 |
| 例 | Whoever comes is welcome. | Whoever comes, I won't open the door. |
| 例 | Take whatever you like. | Whatever you say, I'm going. |

## 例句

- **Whether the plan succeeds depends largely on public support.** 计划能否成功很大程度上取决于公众支持。
- **The committee has not decided whether to extend the deadline.** 委员会还没决定是否延长截止日期。
- **We had a long debate about whether social media does more harm than good.** 我们就社交媒体是否弊大于利进行了长时间的辩论。
- **Could you check if the file has been saved?** 你能检查一下文件是否已经保存了吗？
- **Whoever designed this building had a great sense of humour.** 设计这栋楼的人一定很有幽默感。
- **You can invite whoever you like to the wedding.** 你想邀请谁来参加婚礼都可以。
- **Whatever she cooks tastes amazing.** 她做什么菜都很好吃。
- **Choose whichever route is less crowded.** 哪条路线人少就走哪条。

## 常见错误

- 误：*It all depends on if we get the funding.*。正：**It all depends on whether we get the funding.**。介词后只能用 whether。
- 误：*No matter who breaks the rule will be punished.*。正：**Whoever breaks the rule will be punished.**。从句作主语时必须用 whoever，no matter who 只能引导状语从句。
- 误：*The prize goes to whomever finishes first.*。正：**The prize goes to whoever finishes first.**。whoever 在从句中作 finishes 的主语，不受介词 to 影响。
- 误：*I haven't decided if or not to go.*。正：**I haven't decided whether or not to go.**。if 不能直接接 or not，也不能接不定式。

## 学习提示

一条安全规则：拿不准时用 whether，它几乎在所有"是否"的位置都正确。判断 whoever 还是 whomever，只看从句内部：把从句单独拿出来，缺主语用 whoever，缺宾语用 whomever。至于 -ever 词和 no matter，先看从句是否充当句子成分：是就用 -ever，是附加的让步意义才可以用 no matter。

=== t01-full-inversion | B2
# 1．完全倒装

完全倒装（full inversion）指把**整个谓语动词**放到主语前面，而不是只提前助动词。它主要用于描写场景、引出新信息，或让句子的焦点落在句末的主语上。汉语里“门口站着一个老人”“远处传来一阵歌声”恰好是这种语序，但中国学习者往往不知道英语什么时候允许这样做，或误把代词主语也倒装。

## 核心规则

- **地点 / 方向副词或介词短语位于句首**，谓语是 come, go, stand, lie, sit, live, hang 等表示位置或移动的不及物动词时，可以完全倒装：On the hill **stood an old church**.
- **here / there / out / in / up / down / away 等副词开头**，常用于口语中引起注意：Here **comes the bus**. Off **went the children**.
- **主语是代词时不倒装**：Here it comes.（不说 *Here comes it*）Away they ran.
- **表语提前**，出于平衡或强调，常见于书面语：**Gone are the days** when a letter took a month. **Such was** his anger that nobody spoke.
- **直接引语之后**，转述部分可倒装：“Sit down,” **said the nurse**. 但代词主语通常不倒装：“Sit down,” she said.
- **there be 结构**本身也是一种倒装，There 后还可用 exist, remain, stand 等动词：There remain several problems.

### 完全倒装与部分倒装对比

| 类型 | 提前的成分 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| 完全倒装 | 整个谓语动词 | Into the room walked a stranger. |
| 部分倒装 | 助动词 / 情态动词 / be | Never have I seen such a mess. |
| 主语为代词 | 不倒装 | Into the room he walked. |
| 表语倒装 | 表语 + be | Happy are those who can laugh at themselves. |

### 使用注意

- 完全倒装主要是**文学、新闻描写**的手法，日常对话中除 Here comes... 外用得不多。
- 倒装后的主语往往较长、是新信息，符合英语“尾重”（end weight）原则。
- 动词必须与**后面的主语**保持一致：On the table **were** two envelopes.

## 例句

- **Here comes the tricky part of the experiment.** 实验最棘手的部分来了。
- **At the end of the corridor hung a faded portrait.** 走廊尽头挂着一幅褪色的肖像。
- **Down came the rain, and everyone ran for cover.** 雨哗地下起来，大家都跑去避雨。
- **Beside the river lived an old fisherman and his cat.** 河边住着一位老渔夫和他的猫。
- **"We're closed," said the woman at the counter.** “我们打烊了。”柜台后的女人说。
- **Gone are the days when phones were only for calls.** 手机只能打电话的日子一去不复返了。
- **Among the guests was a famous architect.** 客人中有一位著名建筑师。
- **Out jumped the cat as soon as I opened the box.** 我一打开箱子，猫就跳了出来。

## 常见错误

- 误：*Here comes he.*。正：**Here he comes.**。主语是人称代词时不倒装，这是完全倒装最重要的例外。
- 误：*On the shelf was three old radios.*。正：**On the shelf were three old radios.**。倒装后谓语仍要和后面的真正主语 radios 一致。
- 误：*In the corner did stand a piano.*。正：**In the corner stood a piano.**。完全倒装直接提前实义动词，不借助 do。
- 误：*There stands on the hill a tower.*（生硬）。正：**On the hill stands a tower.**。地点状语提前再倒装更自然，汉语“山上立着一座塔”正好对应这种语序。

## 学习提示

判断能否完全倒装，可以问三个问题：句首是不是地点或方向？动词是不是表示位置、移动的不及物动词？主语是不是名词而非代词？三个都满足，倒装就自然。阅读英文小说时留意 In came... / Up went... 这类句子，体会它们带来的画面感和节奏感。

=== t02-partial-inversion | B2
# 2．部分倒装

部分倒装（partial inversion）只把**助动词、情态动词或 be** 放到主语前面，语序类似疑问句。它是英语正式文体、演讲和考试中的高频考点，常由否定词、only 结构、so / such 或省略 if 的条件句触发。中国学习者最常出错的是：句首用了 never 却忘记倒装，或者把实义动词直接提到主语前面。

## 核心规则

### 1．否定或半否定词开头

- never, seldom, rarely, hardly, scarcely, little, nowhere, at no time, in no way, not until, not only 等：**Rarely do we** get snow here.
- **Hardly / Scarcely ... when** 与 **No sooner ... than**（“一……就……”），主句多用过去完成时：No sooner **had I** sat down than the phone rang.
- **Not until** 置于句首时，倒装发生在**主句**：Not until midnight **did he** finish.
- **Not only ... but (also)**：只有 not only 部分倒装。

### 2．only + 状语开头

- Only then / Only in this way / Only when... **倒装主句**：Only when you try **will you** know.
- only 修饰主语时不倒装：Only Lisa knew the answer.

### 3．so / such 开头

- **So + 形容词 / 副词 ... that**：So quickly **did she** speak that...
- **Such + be + 主语 + that**：Such was the noise that we left.
- **so / neither / nor + 助动词 + 主语**，表示“……也是 / 也不”：I can swim, and **so can my son**.

### 4．省略 if 的条件句

- **Had / Should / Were** 提前：**Had I known**, I would have helped. **Should you need** anything, call me.

| 触发词 | 倒装位置 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| Never / Seldom | 紧随其后 | Seldom does he complain. |
| Not until + 从句 | 主句 | Not until he left did I relax. |
| Only + 状语 | 主句 | Only then did I understand. |
| No sooner | 紧随其后，配 than | No sooner had we arrived than it rained. |
| Had / Were / Should | 句首，代替 if | Were I you, I'd wait. |

## 例句

- **Never have I met such a patient teacher.** 我从未见过如此耐心的老师。
- **Not only did he lose the match, but he also injured his knee.** 他不仅输了比赛，还伤了膝盖。
- **Only by working together can we solve this problem.** 只有通力合作，我们才能解决这个问题。
- **Hardly had the film started when the power went out.** 电影刚开始就停电了。
- **Should you have any questions, please contact our office.** 如果您有任何问题，请联系我们的办公室。
- **So tired was she that she fell asleep on the sofa.** 她累得在沙发上睡着了。
- **At no time did the company admit any wrongdoing.** 公司在任何时候都没有承认有过错。
- **My brother doesn't eat meat, and neither does my sister.** 我哥哥不吃肉，我姐姐也不吃。

## 常见错误

- 误：*Never I have seen such a view.*。正：**Never have I seen such a view.**。否定词位于句首时，助动词必须提到主语之前。
- 误：*Not until he came home he realised the truth.*。正：**Not until he came home did he realise the truth.**。倒装发生在主句，从句 he came home 保持正常语序。
- 误：*Only when I left did I noticed the mistake.*。正：**Only when I left did I notice the mistake.**。借助 did 倒装后，实义动词用原形。
- 误：*No sooner had I arrived when it began to snow.*。正：**No sooner had I arrived than it began to snow.**。no sooner 配 than，hardly / scarcely 才配 when。
- 误：*I like tea, and so like my wife.*。正：**I like tea, and so does my wife.**。so 后用助动词，不能直接提前实义动词。

## 学习提示

把部分倒装看成“把陈述句变成一般疑问句的语序”：先写出 I have never seen...，再把 never 挪到句首，剩下部分按疑问句 have I seen 排列即可。写作中每篇文章用一两次就足以体现语法功底，滥用反而显得做作；口语里 so do I / neither can I 才是最常用的倒装。

=== t03-cleft-sentences | B1
# 3．强调句：It is...that 与 what 分裂句

分裂句（cleft sentence）把一个简单句“拆”成两部分，用来突出其中某个成分，相当于汉语的“正是……”“……的是……”。最常见的有 **It is ... that** 强调句和 **What ... is ...** 分裂句。中国学习者常把强调句和 it 作形式主语的句子混淆，或者在强调人时用错连接词、搞错时态。

## 核心规则

### It is / was ... that / who

- 结构：**It + be + 被强调部分 + that / who + 句子其余部分**。
- 可强调主语、宾语、状语，但**不能强调谓语动词**。
- 强调人时可用 **who**，其余一般用 **that**；强调时间、地点时也用 that，不用 when / where。
- be 的时态看原句：原句是过去时，常用 **It was**。
- 检验方法：去掉 It is 和 that 后，剩下的部分仍是完整的句子。

### What 分裂句（伪分裂句）

- 结构：**What + 从句 + be + 被强调部分**：What I need **is** a long holiday.
- 强调**动作**时用 do：What she did **was (to) call** the police.
- 也可以用 **All / The thing / The reason why / The place where** 替换 what：All I want is some peace.

### 强调谓语动词

- 用助动词 **do / does / did + 动词原形**：I **did** lock the door!

| 原句：Anna found the keys in the car yesterday. | 强调句 |
|---|---|
| 强调主语 | It was Anna who found the keys in the car. |
| 强调宾语 | It was the keys that Anna found in the car. |
| 强调地点 | It was in the car that Anna found the keys. |
| 强调时间 | It was yesterday that Anna found the keys. |
| what 分裂句 | What Anna found in the car was the keys. |

### 与形式主语句的区别

It is important that we start now. 去掉 It is...that 后，important we start now 不成句，所以这是形式主语句，不是强调句。

## 例句

- **It was my grandmother who taught me to cook.** 正是我奶奶教会了我做饭。
- **It is not the price that worries me but the quality.** 让我担心的不是价格，而是质量。
- **It was in Lisbon that they first met.** 他们正是在里斯本初次相遇的。
- **What surprised everyone was his calm reaction.** 让所有人吃惊的是他平静的反应。
- **What we need now is a clear plan.** 我们现在需要的是一个清晰的计划。
- **All I did was ask a simple question.** 我只不过是问了一个简单的问题。
- **Was it you who left the window open?** 是你没关窗户吗？
- **I do remember sending that email.** 我确实记得发过那封邮件。

## 常见错误

- 误：*It was in 2019 when I moved to Shanghai.*。正：**It was in 2019 that I moved to Shanghai.**。强调时间状语时用 that，不用 when。
- 误：*It is my parents who supports me.*。正：**It is my parents who support me.**。who 后的动词与被强调的 parents 保持一致。
- 误：*What I want are some advice.*。正：**What I want is some advice.**。advice 不可数，而且 what 从句作主语通常接单数 is。
- 误：*It was yesterday that I have met her.*。正：**It was yesterday that I met her.**。有 yesterday 这类过去时间，从句用一般过去时。
- 误：*It is who can help you is Mr Lee.*。正：**It is Mr Lee who can help you.**。被强调部分紧跟在 It is 之后。

## 学习提示

汉语“正是……”几乎都可以翻译成 It is ... that / who；“……的是……”则对应 What ... is ...。考试中辨别强调句，就用“去掉 It is 和 that 看剩下的是否完整”这一招。口语中表示反驳、纠正时，What I meant was... 和 It was Tom, not me, who... 非常地道。

=== t04-ellipsis | B2
# 4．省略

省略（ellipsis）是指在上下文清楚的情况下，把重复或可推知的成分省去，使语言简洁、避免啰嗦。英语省略有严格的规律：一般要**保留助动词**作为“占位符”，而汉语可以直接省掉整个谓语（“我去，他也去”）。因此中国学习者常见的问题是省过了头，丢掉了必须保留的助动词、to 或 be。

## 核心规则

### 1．并列句中的省略

- 相同的主语、谓语可以省略：Sara plays the violin and (Sara) sings. / I ordered fish and my friend (ordered) pasta.

### 2．动词短语省略，保留助动词

- 回答或对比时只保留助动词 / 情态动词：Can you drive? — Yes, I **can**. / She has finished, but I **haven't**.
- 前面是实义动词时用 **do / does / did** 替代：He likes jazz and so **does** she.

### 3．不定式省略，保留 to

- want, hope, try, need, would like, be going to 等之后：I didn't go, but I **wanted to**.
- 若 to 后是 be 或 have（助动词），常保留：She isn't a leader, but she **wants to be**.

### 4．状语从句中的省略

- when, while, if, unless, although, once, until 引导的从句，**主语与主句一致且含 be 时**，可省略主语和 be：**While (I was) waiting**, I read the news. **If (it is) necessary**, call me.
- 常见固定表达：if possible, if so, if not, when necessary, as usual, as agreed。

### 5．比较句中的省略

- She runs faster than I (do). / He earns more than (he did) last year.

| 省略类型 | 保留成分 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| 动词短语省略 | 助动词 / 情态动词 | I haven't read it, but Jo has. |
| 不定式省略 | to | You can stay if you want to. |
| 状语从句省略 | 连词 + 分词 / 形容词 | When asked, he refused. |
| 名词省略 | 限定词 / 所有格 | This book is mine, not Tom's. |
| 回答中省略 | 关键词 | Where to? — The airport. |

### 口语中的句首省略

- 非正式口语常省掉句首的主语或助动词：(Do you) Want a coffee? (I) Hope so. (It) Sounds good. 这种省略不宜用于正式写作。

## 例句

- **I was going to call you, but I forgot to.** 我本来要给你打电话的，结果忘了。
- **Some students chose history, others geography.** 有的学生选了历史，有的选了地理。
- **"Has the parcel arrived?" "Not yet, but it should soon."** “包裹到了吗？”“还没有，不过应该快了。”
- **When asked about the delay, the spokesman said nothing.** 被问及延误时，发言人一言不发。
- **Although exhausted, the volunteers kept working.** 尽管筋疲力尽，志愿者们仍在工作。
- **Take an umbrella if necessary.** 如有必要，就带把伞。
- **My sister reads more than I do.** 我姐姐读的书比我多。
- **I'd love to join you, but I can't.** 我很想和你们一起去，可是去不了。

## 常见错误

- 误：*He can speak French, and I also.*。正：**He can speak French, and so can I.**。英语省略要保留助动词，也可以说 and I can too。
- 误：*I wanted to help, but I didn't have time to it.*。正：**I wanted to help, but I didn't have time to.**。不定式省略只保留 to，不加代词 it。
- 误：*While walking home, the rain started.*。正：**While I was walking home, the rain started.**。只有主从句主语一致时才能省略，否则会出现“雨在走路”的逻辑错误。
- 误：*She is taller than me am.*。正：**She is taller than I am.**。保留助动词时必须用主格 I；口语中也可以只说 taller than me。
- 误：*"Will you come?" "Yes, I will come to."*。正：**"Will you come?" "Yes, I will."**。简短回答只保留助动词。

## 学习提示

记住一句话：英语“可以省内容，不能省骨架”。助动词、to、限定词就是骨架，能帮读者找回被省略的部分。翻译“我也是 / 我也不”时，先判断前一句的助动词是什么（can, did, have, am），再套 so / neither + 助动词 + 主语，就不会写出 *me too can* 之类的句子。

=== t05-substitution | C1
# 5．替代：so、not、do so、one

替代（substitution）是用一个“代用词”来代替前文出现过的名词、动词短语或整个从句，它与省略一起构成英语语篇衔接的重要手段。英语极力避免原词重复，而汉语对重复的容忍度高得多，所以中国学习者的文章常常显得啰嗦（*I think it will rain. Yes, I think it will rain too.*）。熟练使用 so、not、do so、one，能让表达更地道、更紧凑。

## 核心规则

### 1．名词替代：one / ones

- **one** 替代前面出现的**可数名词单数**，**ones** 替代复数：I lost my pen, so I bought a new **one**.
- one 前可以有形容词或限定词：the red one, this one, the ones on the left。
- **不可数名词不能用 one 替代**，要直接省略或用 some：I need milk. — There's **some** in the fridge.
- 所有格之后不用 one：That car is **Mark's**（不说 *Mark's one*）。
- **that / those** 用于正式比较中替代前面的名词：The climate here is milder than **that** of the north.

### 2．从句替代：so / not

- 在 think, believe, suppose, expect, hope, guess, be afraid, imagine 之后，**so** 替代肯定的 that 从句，**not** 替代否定从句：Will it rain? — I hope **not**.
- think, believe, expect 的否定通常说 **I don't think so**，而 hope 和 be afraid 只能说 **I hope not / I'm afraid not**。
- **if so / if not** 在正式写作中很常用。

### 3．动词短语替代：do / do so / do it / do that

- **do** 用于简单替代（常见于比较或并列）：She sings better than I **do**.
- **do so** 更正式，常用于书面语，替代有意图的动作：The board asked him to resign, and he **did so** the next day.
- **do it / do that** 口语中常用，that 带强调或对比。
- 表示状态的动词（know, like, own）一般不用 do so 替代。

| 代用词 | 替代对象 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| one / ones | 可数名词 | I prefer the blue ones. |
| that / those | 正式比较中的名词 | Prices now are higher than those of 2020. |
| so / not | that 从句 | I believe so. / I suspect not. |
| do / do so | 动词短语 | If you wish to leave, please do so quietly. |
| so（句首） | 表示赞同的事实 | "It's late." "So it is." |

### 容易混淆的 So I do 与 So do I

- **So do I** = 我也是（倒装，指另一个人）；**So I do** / So it is = 确实如此（不倒装，对事实表示惊讶或确认）。

## 例句

- **This laptop is too heavy; I need a lighter one.** 这台笔记本太重了，我需要一台轻一点的。
- **"Is the museum open on Mondays?" "I'm afraid not."** “博物馆周一开放吗？”“恐怕不开。”
- **"Will the train be late?" "I expect so."** “火车会晚点吗？”“我想会的。”
- **Applicants who wish to withdraw may do so before Friday.** 希望撤回申请的申请人可于周五前撤回。
- **The population of Tokyo is larger than that of London.** 东京的人口比伦敦多。
- **Check whether the file has been saved and, if not, save it again.** 检查文件是否已保存，如果没有，就再存一次。
- **The old bridges were replaced by stronger ones.** 旧桥被更坚固的桥取代了。
- **"You've spilled coffee on your shirt." "So I have!"** “你把咖啡洒到衬衫上了。”“还真是！”

## 常见错误

- 误：*I don't have a car, but I want to buy it.*。正：**I don't have a car, but I want to buy one.**。it 指特定的那一辆，泛指“一辆”要用 one。
- 误：*I hope not so.*。正：**I hope not.**。not 本身就替代了否定从句，不能与 so 连用。
- 误：*I like hot coffee more than cold one.*。正：**I like hot coffee more than cold.**。coffee 在这里不可数，不能用 one 替代，直接省略名词即可。
- 误：*The weather in June is hotter than May.*。正：**The weather in June is hotter than that in May.**。比较对象要对等，用 that 替代 the weather。
- 误：*"Is he coming?" "I don't think."*。正：**"Is he coming?" "I don't think so."**。think 后的宾语从句要用 so 替代，不能空着。

## 学习提示

写完一段英文后，圈出所有重复出现的名词和动词短语，逐一问自己：能否换成 one、that、so 或 do so？这是提升写作“地道感”最快的方法之一。雅思和托福写作中，“that of + 名词”的比较结构尤其能体现语言的严谨，但也要注意别让代用词离所指对象太远，导致指代不清。

=== t06-negation | C1
# 6．否定的特殊形式：部分否定、双重否定与否定转移

英语的否定远不止在动词后加 not。**部分否定**、**双重否定**、**否定转移**以及“形式肯定、意义否定”的表达，常常让读者误解作者本意。汉语“所有人都不同意”与“不是所有人都同意”分得很清楚，而英语的 All ... not 却可能是部分否定，这正是中国学习者在阅读理解和翻译中频频失分的原因。

## 核心规则

### 1．部分否定

- **all, both, every, everybody, always, entirely, completely, quite** 与 **not** 连用，通常表示“并非都 / 并不总是”：**Not all** students passed.（并非所有学生都及格了）
- 表示**全部否定**要用 **none, neither, no one, nothing, never**：**None of** the students passed.
- **All ... not** 语序有歧义，写作中应改用 **Not all ...** 表示部分否定。

| 部分否定 | 全部否定 |
|---|---|
| Not all of them agreed. | None of them agreed. |
| I don't know both of them. | I know neither of them. |
| He is not always right. | He is never right. |
| Not everything is for sale. | Nothing is for sale. |

### 2．双重否定

- 两个否定相互抵消，表示**肯定**，语气往往更委婉或更强调：It is **not uncommon** to see foxes in the city.（相当常见）
- 常见结构：not un- / not in-，no ... without，never ... but，cannot help doing，cannot ... too（怎么……也不为过）。
- 标准英语中 *I don't know nothing* 这种“否定叠加”是不规范的，应说 I don't know anything。

### 3．否定转移

- think, believe, suppose, expect, imagine, seem, appear 等后接否定意义的从句时，英语习惯把 not 放到**主句**：**I don't think** he's coming.（而不是 I think he isn't coming）
- 反义疑问句要跟从句：I don't think she's ready, **is she**?
- hope 和 fear 不转移：I hope he won't be late.

### 4．意义否定

- **too ... to**（太……而不能），**anything but**（绝不），**far from**（远非），**the last person to**（最不可能……的人），**fail to**（未能）。
- **hardly, barely, scarcely, seldom** 本身含否定义，不再与 not 连用。

## 例句

- **Not every expensive restaurant serves good food.** 并非每家昂贵的餐厅都提供好吃的菜。
- **I don't think this plan will work.** 我认为这个计划行不通。
- **None of the witnesses could describe the driver.** 没有一个目击者能描述出司机的样子。
- **It is not unusual for flights to be delayed in winter.** 冬天航班延误并不少见。
- **You cannot be too careful when handling chemicals.** 处理化学品时再小心也不为过。
- **The explanation was far from clear.** 这个解释远不清楚。
- **She is the last person to break a promise.** 她绝不是那种会食言的人。
- **I could hardly hear what he was saying.** 我几乎听不见他在说什么。

## 常见错误

- 误：*All the answers are not correct.*（想表达“答案全错”）。正：**None of the answers are correct.**。All ... not 通常被理解为部分否定，全部否定要用 none。
- 误：*I think she doesn't like the gift.*（不自然）。正：**I don't think she likes the gift.**。英语习惯否定转移，把 not 放在 think 上。
- 误：*I can't hardly believe it.*。正：**I can hardly believe it.**。hardly 本身已表否定，再加 not 构成不规范的双重否定。
- 误：*He didn't say nothing to me.*。正：**He didn't say anything to me.**。标准英语一个分句只用一个否定词。
- 误：*I don't think he is honest, do I?*。正：**I don't think he is honest, is he?**。否定转移句的反义疑问部分与从句主谓保持一致。

## 学习提示

阅读时一看到 all、both、every、always 与 not 同时出现，就停下来问自己“是全部不，还是不全是”，大多数情况下是后者。翻译成汉语时“不都是”“并非总是”要体现出来。写作中用 not uncommon、not without reason 这类双重否定，能让语气更克制、更学术，但一段之内用一次就够了。

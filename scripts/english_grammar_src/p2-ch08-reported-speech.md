=== t01-reported-statements | B1
# 1．陈述句的转述与时态后移

直接引语（direct speech）原封不动地引用别人的话，放在引号里；间接引语（reported speech / indirect speech）则用自己的话转述内容，通常变成 that 引导的宾语从句。汉语转述别人的话时动词不变形，所以中国学习者最常见的问题是：主句用了过去时，从句却忘了“时态后移”（backshift），或者人称、时间、地点状语没有随说话场景一起调整。

## 核心规则

- 转述动词为**过去时**（said, told）时，从句时态一般**往过去方向退一步**。
- 转述动词为**现在时**（says, has said, will say）时，从句时态**不变**：She says she **is** busy.
- **that** 在口语中常省略：He said (that) he was tired.
- **say** 后不直接接人；**tell** 后必须接人：said to me / told me。
- 若所述内容**现在仍然真实**或是客观真理，时态可以不后移：He told us the museum **opens** at nine.（今天仍如此）
- 过去完成时、would、could、should、might、ought to 等本身已无法再后移，保持不变。

### 时态后移对照表

| 直接引语 | 间接引语 |
|---|---|
| 一般现在时 am / do | 一般过去时 was / did |
| 现在进行时 is doing | 过去进行时 was doing |
| 现在完成时 has done | 过去完成时 had done |
| 一般过去时 did | 过去完成时 had done（口语中也可不变） |
| will / can / may | would / could / might |
| must（义务） | had to 或 must 不变 |

### 指示语的变化

| 直接引语 | 间接引语 |
|---|---|
| now | then / at that time |
| today / tonight | that day / that night |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| here / this | there / that |
| ago | before |

这些变化并非机械规则：如果转述时仍在同一天、同一地点，就不必改。关键是站在“转述者此刻”的角度重新定位时间和空间。

## 例句

- **Mia said she was looking for a cheaper flat.** 米娅说她在找一套更便宜的公寓。
- **He told me he had already sent the report.** 他告诉我他已经把报告发出去了。
- **The coach said we would train indoors the next day.** 教练说我们第二天在室内训练。
- **My sister says she can't come to dinner tonight.** 我姐姐说她今晚来不了吃晚饭。
- **Our teacher explained that light travels faster than sound.** 老师解释说光比声音传播得快。
- **Tom told us he had lived in Kyoto two years before.** 汤姆告诉我们，他两年前在京都住过。
- **She said that she might change her mind.** 她说她可能会改变主意。
- **The manager said the shop closes at ten on weekdays.** 经理说这家店工作日十点关门。（现在仍如此）

## 常见错误

- 误：*He said me that he was busy.*。正：**He told me that he was busy.**。say 后不能直接跟人作宾语，要用 tell sb. 或 say to sb.。
- 误：*She said she will call me tomorrow.*。正：**She said she would call me the next day.**。主句为过去时，will 应后移为 would，时间状语也要随之调整。
- 误：*Jack said that I was very tired.*（Jack 在说自己）。正：**Jack said that he was very tired.**。人称要从说话人的角度改成第三人称，汉语里常直接照搬“我”。
- 误：*He told that he lost his keys.*。正：**He said that he had lost his keys.**。tell 必须带人称宾语；丢钥匙发生在说话之前，用过去完成时更准确。
- 误：*She said, she was hungry.*。正：**She said she was hungry.**。间接引语不用逗号和引号隔开。

## 学习提示

记住口诀“主句过去，从句退一步；人称跟着说话人换，时间地点跟着现场换”。考试中遇到客观真理（地球绕太阳转、水在 100℃ 沸腾）时，从句保持一般现在时；遇到 said / told 却看到 will、can，多半就是要改成 would、could 的考点。写作中转述访谈、报道时，时态后移能让叙事显得连贯、专业。

=== t02-reported-questions-commands | B1
# 2．疑问句与祈使句的转述

转述问题和命令时，句子结构要发生比陈述句更大的变化：疑问句要变成**陈述语序**的宾语从句，祈使句要变成**动词 + 宾语 + 不定式**。中国学习者最常犯的错误是在间接疑问句里保留疑问语序（*asked where was the station*），或者在句尾照样打问号。

## 核心规则

### 一般疑问句

- 用 **if / whether** 引导，语序改为**主语 + 谓语**，去掉助动词 do / does / did。
- 句末用句号，不用问号。
- whether 更正式，并可以直接接 **or not**：She asked whether or not I was coming.

### 特殊疑问句

- 保留原疑问词 **what / where / why / how** 等，后接陈述语序。
- 若疑问词本身作主语，语序本来就是陈述语序：“Who broke it?” → She asked who had broken it.

### 祈使句

- 命令、要求：**tell / order / ask sb. (not) to do**。
- 请求：**ask sb. to do**；建议：**advise / remind / warn sb. to do**。
- 否定祈使句用 **not to do**：Don't be late. → He told us **not to be** late.

### 句型对照

| 直接引语 | 间接引语 |
|---|---|
| "Are you ready?" | She asked if I was ready. |
| "Where do you work?" | He asked where I worked. |
| "Who called?" | She asked who had called. |
| "Close the door." | He told me to close the door. |
| "Please don't shout." | She asked us not to shout. |
| "Shall I help?" | He asked if he should help. / He offered to help. |

### 其他细节

- 疑问句转述的动词常用 **ask, wonder, want to know, inquire**（正式）。
- 礼貌问句 Could you...? 通常转述为请求：She asked me to lend her a pen.
- 转述动词为过去时，同样要做时态后移。

## 例句

- **The officer asked whether I had anything to declare.** 海关官员问我是否有东西要申报。
- **I wondered why the lights were still on.** 我纳闷为什么灯还亮着。
- **She asked me how long I had been learning Spanish.** 她问我学西班牙语多久了。
- **The doctor told him to drink more water.** 医生叫他多喝水。
- **Our landlord asked us not to leave bikes in the hallway.** 房东请我们不要把自行车放在走廊里。
- **They wanted to know when the results would be published.** 他们想知道结果什么时候公布。
- **He asked who was responsible for the budget.** 他问谁负责预算。
- **My mother reminded me to lock the back door.** 妈妈提醒我锁好后门。

## 常见错误

- 误：*She asked me where did I live.*。正：**She asked me where I lived.**。间接疑问句要用陈述语序，去掉 did。
- 误：*He asked me that if I liked jazz.*。正：**He asked me if I liked jazz.**。if / whether 已经是连接词，前面不能再加 that。
- 误：*The teacher told us don't talk.*。正：**The teacher told us not to talk.**。否定祈使句转述为 not to do，not 放在 to 前面。
- 误：*I asked him could he help me?*。正：**I asked him if he could help me.**。改为陈述语序并去掉问号；也可以说 I asked him to help me.
- 误：*She said me to wait outside.*。正：**She told me to wait outside.**。say 不接“人 + 不定式”结构。

## 学习提示

检查间接疑问句只需一招：把从句单独拿出来读，如果读起来像一个陈述句（where I lived），就对了；如果像一个问题（where did I live），就错了。这个规则同样适用于 Do you know where the station is? 这类“嵌入式问句”，在口语和雅思、托福写作中都非常高频。

=== t03-reporting-verbs | B2
# 3．转述动词及其句型

只会用 say 和 tell 转述，文章会显得单调，也无法表达说话人的意图和态度。英语有大量**转述动词**（reporting verbs），如 admit, deny, insist, suggest, refuse, blame，它们不仅概括了“说了什么”，还体现了“怎么说、为什么说”。难点在于每个动词都有固定句型，中国学习者常把 suggest 后接不定式，或给 explain 直接加人称宾语。

## 核心句型分类

| 句型 | 常用动词 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| 动词 + to do | agree, refuse, offer, promise, threaten, decide | She refused to sign. |
| 动词 + sb. + to do | advise, warn, urge, remind, persuade, invite, encourage | He urged us to vote. |
| 动词 + doing | admit, deny, suggest, recommend, mention | He denied taking it. |
| 动词 + 介词 + doing | apologise for, insist on, object to, confess to | She insisted on paying. |
| 动词 + sb. + 介词 + doing | accuse sb. of, blame sb. for, thank sb. for, congratulate sb. on | They blamed me for the delay. |
| 动词 + that 从句 | admit, claim, explain, argue, point out, complain, announce | He claimed that it was a mistake. |
| 动词 + (that) + should / 原形 | suggest, recommend, insist, demand, propose | She suggested that we leave early. |

## 要点与辨析

- **suggest / recommend** 不能接 sb. to do：*suggest him to go* 是错误的，要用 suggest going 或 suggest that he (should) go。
- **explain / describe / announce** 若要提到听者，用 **to sb.**：explain to me that...。
- **insist** 表示“坚持要求”时从句用虚拟（should）原形；表示“坚持认为”时用正常时态：He insisted that he **had been** at home.
- 同一个动词可能有多种句型，但语义不同：**remember to do**（记得要做）与 **remember doing**（记得做过）在转述中也要注意。
- 语气强弱：claim（声称，暗含怀疑）→ state（陈述，中性）→ confirm（证实）。学术写作中 argue, point out, suggest, note 用得最多。
- 转述动词本身也是一种“总结”：“I'm sorry I broke it.” 可以直接转述为 He apologised for breaking it.，无需保留原话细节。

## 例句

- **The minister denied having any links to the company.** 部长否认与该公司有任何联系。
- **My tutor advised me to narrow down my research question.** 导师建议我缩小研究问题的范围。
- **She apologised for missing the deadline.** 她为错过截止日期道歉。
- **The neighbours complained that the music was too loud.** 邻居抱怨音乐太吵。
- **He threatened to cancel the contract.** 他威胁要取消合同。
- **The report suggests that the policy has had little effect.** 报告表明这项政策收效甚微。
- **They accused the driver of ignoring the signal.** 他们指责司机无视信号。
- **I persuaded my brother to apply for the scholarship.** 我说服哥哥去申请奖学金。

## 常见错误

- 误：*The teacher suggested us to read more.*。正：**The teacher suggested that we read more.**。suggest 不接“人 + 不定式”，可用 that 从句或 suggested reading more。
- 误：*He explained me the rules.*。正：**He explained the rules to me.**。explain 不能接双宾语，受益者用 to sb.。
- 误：*She denied to take the money.*。正：**She denied taking the money.**。deny 后接动名词，不接不定式。
- 误：*They accused him for cheating.*。正：**They accused him of cheating.**。accuse 固定搭配 of；blame 才用 for。
- 误：*I insisted to pay the bill.*。正：**I insisted on paying the bill.**。insist 表“坚持做”时用 insist on doing。

## 学习提示

建议把转述动词按句型做成卡片，每张卡片只写“动词 + 句型 + 一个自己的例句”，一次记整条搭配而不是单个词。写议论文引用观点时，用 argue、claim、point out、acknowledge 代替反复出现的 say，并注意 claim 带有“未必可信”的色彩——选择转述动词本身就是在表态。

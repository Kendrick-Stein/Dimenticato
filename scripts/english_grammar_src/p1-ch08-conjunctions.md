=== t01-coordinating | A1
# 1．并列连词 and / but / or / so

并列连词（coordinating conjunctions）连接两个语法地位相同的成分：单词与单词、短语与短语、分句与分句。最常用的是 and、but、or、so，完整的一组可用缩写 **FANBOYS** 记忆（for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so）。中国学习者常见的问题是"虽然……但是"式的 although...but 连用，以及用逗号直接连接两个完整句子。

## 核心规则

### 一、四个核心并列连词

| 连词 | 关系 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| and | 并列、补充、顺序 | I washed the dishes and dried them. |
| but | 转折、对比 | It's small but comfortable. |
| or | 选择；否定句中的"也不" | Tea or coffee? / She doesn't eat meat or fish. |
| so | 结果 | It was raining, so we stayed in. |

### 二、其他并列连词

- **yet**：然而（比 but 正式，常带"出人意料"）：*The plan was simple, yet it worked.*
- **for**：因为（书面、文学化，只能连接分句，不能放句首）。
- **nor**：也不（常用于否定句之后，后接倒装）：*He didn't call, **nor** did he write.*

### 三、标点规则

- 连接两个**完整分句**时，连词前通常加**逗号**：*I was tired, **but** I finished the report.* 两句都很短时可省略逗号。
- 连接**单词或短语**时一般不加逗号：*bread and butter*。
- 列举三项以上：*apples, oranges(,) and pears*，最后一个逗号称为"牛津逗号"，美式写作常用，英式可省略。
- **不能只用逗号**连接两个完整句子（comma splice）：*It was late, we went home.* 是错误的。

### 四、与汉语的差异

- 汉语"虽然……但是""因为……所以"成对出现，英语中 **although 与 but、because 与 so 不能同时使用**，二选一。
- 否定句中表示"和"要用 **or**：*I don't like tea **or** coffee.*
- 祈使句 + and / or 表示条件：*Hurry up, **or** you'll miss the train.*（否则）/ *Work hard **and** you'll succeed.*（那么）

## 例句

- **My brother likes football, and my sister prefers tennis.** 我哥哥喜欢足球，我姐姐更喜欢网球。
- **The restaurant was expensive but the food was excellent.** 那家餐厅很贵，但菜很棒。
- **Would you like to sit inside or outside?** 你想坐里面还是外面？
- **I missed the last bus, so I took a taxi.** 我错过了末班车，所以打了车。
- **He doesn't smoke or drink.** 他不抽烟也不喝酒。
- **Put your coat on, or you'll catch a cold.** 穿上外套，不然会感冒的。
- **She was exhausted, yet she kept on working.** 她已经精疲力尽，却仍在工作。
- **We cleaned the kitchen, did the laundry and walked the dog.** 我们打扫了厨房、洗了衣服，还遛了狗。

## 常见错误

- 误：*Although it was cold, but we went swimming.*。正：**Although it was cold, we went swimming.** 或 **It was cold, but we went swimming.**。although 与 but 不能同时使用，这是汉语"虽然……但是"的负迁移。
- 误：*Because I was ill, so I stayed at home.*。正：**Because I was ill, I stayed at home.**。because 和 so 只能保留一个。
- 误：*I don't like cats and dogs.*。正：**I don't like cats or dogs.**。否定句中表示"都不"用 or；用 and 可能被理解为"不喜欢猫狗同时出现"。
- 误：*It was late, we decided to go home.*。正：**It was late, so we decided to go home.**。两个完整句子不能只用逗号连接，需要加连词或改用句号。

## 学习提示

写作时检查每个逗号：如果逗号两边都是能独立成句的完整句子，逗号后必须有 and、but、so 等连词。记住"英语连词只用一个"：汉语习惯的"虽然……但是""因为……所以"在英语中都要砍掉一半。

=== t02-correlative | B1
# 2．关联连词 both...and、either...or、not only...but also

关联连词（correlative conjunctions）由两部分组成，成对使用，把两个并列成分联系起来，如 both...and、either...or、neither...nor、not only...but also、whether...or。它们能让表达更有逻辑、更有力。使用时有两大难点：一是**平行结构**，两部分后接的成分必须词性和结构一致；二是作主语时**谓语动词的单复数**。

## 核心规则

### 一、常见关联连词

| 结构 | 意思 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| both A and B | A 和 B 都 | Both Anna and Ben are coming. |
| either A or B | 要么 A 要么 B | You can either call or text me. |
| neither A nor B | 既不 A 也不 B | Neither the hotel nor the food was good. |
| not only A but (also) B | 不仅 A 而且 B | She not only sings but also dances. |
| not A but B | 不是 A 而是 B | It's not red but orange. |
| whether A or B | 无论 A 还是 B / 是 A 还是 B | Whether you agree or not, I'm going. |

### 二、平行结构

两个连接部分必须语法地位相同：名词对名词、动词对动词、介词短语对介词短语。

- 正：*She is **both** clever **and** hard-working.*（形容词对形容词）
- 误：*She **both** is clever **and** hard-working.*（both 放错了位置）

关键是把关联词的第一部分**紧挨**所连接的第一个成分放置。

### 三、主谓一致

- **both A and B** 作主语，谓语用**复数**。
- **either...or、neither...nor、not only...but also** 遵循**就近原则**，谓语与 **B** 一致：*Neither the students nor the teacher **was** ready. / Either you or your brother **has** to go.*
- 为避免别扭，常把复数名词放在后面：*Neither the teacher nor the students **were** ready.*

### 四、not only 置于句首的倒装

**Not only** 放在句首连接分句时，第一个分句要**部分倒装**：*Not only **did she** win the race, but she (also) broke the record.*

### 五、否定的注意事项

- **both** 不用于否定句，"两者都不"要说 **neither...nor** 或 **not...either**：*I don't like either of them.*
- **neither...nor** 已含否定，不再与 not 连用。

## 例句

- **Both my parents and my grandparents live in the same city.** 我父母和祖父母都住在同一座城市。
- **You can pay either in cash or by card.** 你可以付现金，也可以刷卡。
- **Neither the manager nor her assistant knew the answer.** 经理和她的助理都不知道答案。
- **The course is not only useful but also enjoyable.** 这门课不仅有用，而且有趣。
- **Not only did he forget my birthday, but he also forgot our anniversary.** 他不仅忘了我的生日，还忘了我们的纪念日。
- **The problem is not the price but the quality.** 问题不在价格，而在质量。
- **Whether we win or lose, we'll learn something.** 无论输赢，我们都会有所收获。
- **Either the children or their father is going to cook tonight.** 今晚要么孩子们做饭，要么他们的爸爸做。

## 常见错误

- 误：*Both Tom and Lucy is from Canada.*。正：**Both Tom and Lucy are from Canada.**。both...and 连接主语时谓语用复数。
- 误：*Neither my sister nor I doesn't like fish.*。正：**Neither my sister nor I like fish.**。neither...nor 本身表示否定，不能再加 not；谓语与就近的 I 一致。
- 误：*She not only speaks French but also Spanish.*。正：**She speaks not only French but also Spanish.**。平行结构要求两部分对称：French 对 Spanish。
- 误：*Not only he is rich, but also he is generous.*。正：**Not only is he rich, but he is also generous.**。not only 置于句首时第一个分句要倒装。

## 学习提示

写关联连词句子时做一个"对称检查"：把两个连接词后面的内容上下对齐，看看它们是不是同一种结构。遇到主语中有 either / neither / not only，就看离动词最近的那个名词来决定单复数，这是语法考试的高频考点。

=== t03-subordinating | B1
# 3．从属连词总览

从属连词（subordinating conjunctions）引导从句，并把从句"挂"在主句上，表明两者之间的时间、原因、条件、让步、目的、结果等逻辑关系。掌握从属连词是写出复杂句的基础。中国学习者常见的问题是：从句单独成句（句子碎片），在同一句中叠用 although 和 but，以及混淆 because / because of 这类连词与介词。

## 核心规则

### 一、按语义分类

| 语义 | 常用从属连词 |
|---|---|
| 时间 | when, while, as, before, after, until, since, as soon as, once |
| 原因 | because, as, since, now that |
| 条件 | if, unless, as long as, provided (that), in case |
| 让步 | although, though, even though, even if, while, whereas |
| 目的 | so that, in order that |
| 结果 | so...that, such...that |
| 比较 | than, as...as |
| 方式 | as, as if, as though |
| 名词性从句 | that, whether, if |

### 二、位置与标点

- 从句在**主句前**时，从句后加**逗号**：*When I got home, the lights were off.*
- 从句在**主句后**时，通常**不加逗号**：*The lights were off when I got home.*
- 从句不能独立成句：*Because I was tired.* 单独写出是句子碎片（fragment）。

### 三、时间和条件从句中的时态

在 when、before、after、until、as soon as、if、unless 等引导的时间和条件状语从句中，**用一般现在时表示将来**：*I'll call you when I **arrive**.*（不说 *when I will arrive*）

### 四、易混连词辨析

- **because / because of**：because 是连词，后接从句；because of 是介词短语，后接名词：*because it rained / because of the rain*。
- **although / despite**：although 接从句；despite / in spite of 接名词或动名词。
- **while**：既可表示"当……时"，也可表示对比"而"：*I like tea, **while** my wife prefers coffee.*
- **since**：既表示"自从"，也表示"既然、因为"。
- **unless** = if...not：*Don't call me **unless** it's urgent.*
- **even if**（即使，假设）与 **even though**（尽管，事实）不同。
- **so that**（目的，常接情态动词）与 **so...that**（结果）不同。

## 例句

- **As soon as the meeting ended, everyone rushed out.** 会议一结束，大家就冲了出去。
- **I'll wait here until you come back.** 我会在这里等你回来。
- **She took the job because the salary was good.** 她接受了这份工作，因为薪水不错。
- **Unless you book early, you won't get a seat.** 如果不早点订，你就没有座位了。
- **Even though he was injured, he finished the match.** 尽管受了伤，他还是打完了比赛。
- **I'm saving money so that I can travel next year.** 我在攒钱，这样明年就能去旅行了。
- **Some people love city life, whereas others prefer the countryside.** 有些人喜欢城市生活，而另一些人更喜欢乡村。
- **Since you're already here, why don't you stay for dinner?** 既然你已经来了，何不留下来吃晚饭？

## 常见错误

- 误：*Although he is young, but he is very wise.*。正：**Although he is young, he is very wise.**。英语中 although 和 but 不能同时出现。
- 误：*I'll text you when I will get there.*。正：**I'll text you when I get there.**。时间状语从句用一般现在时表将来。
- 误：*We cancelled the trip because the storm.*。正：**We cancelled the trip because of the storm.**。the storm 是名词，要用介词短语 because of。
- 误：*I like her. Because she is kind.*。正：**I like her because she is kind.**。because 从句不能独立成句，须与主句相连。

## 学习提示

把从属连词按"时间—原因—条件—让步—目的"五大类整理成思维导图，写作时根据逻辑关系挑选，而不是只会用 and 和 but。一个简单检验：从句能否单独回答问题？"Why?"—"Because I was tired." 在对话中可以，写作中则必须与主句相连。

=== t04-linking-adverbs | B2
# 4．连接副词 however / therefore 与标点

连接副词（conjunctive adverbs，也称连接状语）如 however、therefore、moreover、nevertheless、consequently，用来表示句子之间的逻辑关系，是学术和正式写作中组织篇章的重要手段。它们在意义上像连词，语法上却是**副词**，不能像 and、but 那样直接连接两个分句。中国学习者写作中最典型的错误就是 *..., however, ...*、*..., therefore ...* 这种用逗号连接两个完整句子的"逗号粘连"（comma splice）。

## 核心规则

### 一、按逻辑关系分类

| 关系 | 连接副词 | 语体 |
|---|---|---|
| 转折 / 对比 | however, nevertheless, nonetheless, on the other hand, in contrast | 中性至正式 |
| 因果 | therefore, consequently, as a result, thus, hence | 正式 |
| 递进 | moreover, furthermore, in addition, besides, what's more | 正式；besides 偏口语 |
| 举例 | for example, for instance | 中性 |
| 替代 | instead, otherwise, rather | 中性 |
| 总结 | in conclusion, overall, in short | 中性 |
| 时间 | meanwhile, subsequently, afterwards | 中性至正式 |

### 二、标点规则（核心）

连接两个完整分句时有三种正确方式：

- **句号**：*The results were promising. **However,** more research is needed.*
- **分号**：*The results were promising; **however,** more research is needed.*
- **句中插入**（两侧逗号）：*The results were promising. More research, **however,** is needed.*

错误方式：*The results were promising, however, more research is needed.*（逗号粘连）

### 三、位置

- **句首**最常见，后接逗号。
- **句中**：放在主语后或助动词后，两侧用逗号：*The team, **however,** did not give up.*
- **句末**：*It was a risk. We took it, **however**.*（口语化）
- **thus / hence** 在正式写作中也可引导分词或名词短语：*The price fell, **thus making** the product more popular.*

### 四、语体与使用频率

- **besides** 和 **what's more** 偏口语；**hence、thus** 非常正式。
- **on the contrary** 表示"恰恰相反"（否定前一观点），不能用来简单对比两件事；对比两者用 **in contrast / on the other hand**。

## 例句

- **The flight was delayed; however, we still arrived in time for the wedding.** 航班延误了，不过我们还是及时赶上了婚礼。
- **The data were incomplete. Therefore, no firm conclusions can be drawn.** 数据不完整，因此无法得出确切结论。
- **The apartment is close to the station. Moreover, the rent is reasonable.** 这套公寓离车站很近，而且租金合理。
- **She had little experience. Nevertheless, she was offered the position.** 她经验不多，但仍获得了这个职位。
- **Many customers, however, preferred the older model.** 然而，许多顾客更喜欢旧款。
- **Sales dropped sharply; as a result, several stores were closed.** 销量大跌，结果几家门店关闭了。
- **Don't rely on memory alone; instead, write everything down.** 不要只靠记忆，而是把一切都写下来。
- **Urban areas are growing rapidly. In contrast, many rural towns are shrinking.** 城市地区迅速扩张；相比之下，许多乡村小镇正在萎缩。

## 常见错误

- 误：*I studied hard, however, I failed the test.*。正：**I studied hard; however, I failed the test.**。however 是副词，不能用逗号连接两个完整分句，需改用分号或句号。
- 误：*The road was icy, therefore we drove slowly.*。正：**The road was icy, so we drove slowly.** 或 **The road was icy; therefore, we drove slowly.**。therefore 不是连词，要么换成 so，要么改用分号。
- 误：*Although the plan was risky, however, they went ahead.*。正：**Although the plan was risky, they went ahead.**。although 与 however 意思重叠，只能保留一个，这也是"虽然……但是"的负迁移。
- 误：*Cats are independent. On the contrary, dogs need a lot of attention.*。正：**Cats are independent. In contrast, dogs need a lot of attention.**。on the contrary 用于否定前面的说法，对比两者用 in contrast。

## 学习提示

写作自查时，把每个 however、therefore、moreover 都圈出来，看它前面是什么标点：如果是逗号，而且逗号前后都是完整的句子，就把逗号改成分号或句号。记住一句口诀："but 和 so 是连词，逗号就够；however 和 therefore 是副词，分号句号来帮忙。"雅思和托福写作中，正确使用连接副词和标点是"连贯与衔接"评分项的关键。

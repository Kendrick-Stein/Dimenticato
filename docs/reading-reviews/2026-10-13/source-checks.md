## French

Source: https://www.inrae.fr/actualites/regle-jauger-innovation-hydrometrique-reconnue-lorganisation-meteorologique-mondiale-omm

Visible original publication: 08 octobre 2026; award ceremony October 7, Amsterdam. Article by Jérôme Le Coz, INRAE researcher, about the institute's own RiverLy team. No translation/reprint credit; supports French institutional provenance.

https://www.inrae.fr/conditions-generales-dutilisation expressly puts publicly visible institutional text under Licence Ouverte Etalab 2.0, subject to separately marked exceptions. No article-body restrictive text notice found. https://www.data.gouv.fr/pages/legal/licences/etalab-2.0 grants adaptation and publication, requires source and latest available update date, and excludes official-endorsement implications. Credit INRAE and Jérôme Le Coz, date, original link, license and modifications. The instrument's open-source status is separate from the article's license. Exclude all images (hero © Vaisala), linked paper/software/plans and separately owned works.

Fact anchors: transparent PVC plate is placed facing the flow; upstream/downstream height difference depends on speed. Calibration gives speed; multiple speed and depth readings across a cross-section give discharge. The article does not say a single height measurement directly gives discharge. Method dates to at least the 1940s; INRAE continued development from 2018 and improved a model in 2020. Normally within 10% of reference discharge in suitable conditions, including speed >20 cm/s; operator error may be larger and training is necessary. More than 700 INRAE rulers are used worldwide. Avoid promising safe unsupervised water entry.
